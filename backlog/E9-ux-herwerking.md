# E9 — UX-herwerking na de directie-review

**Phase:** 3 (parallel to E4/E5) · **Milestone:** none of its own — it repairs M2/M3 screens that already ship
**Goal:** Make the built screens usable by the teachers who will actually use them. Five change requests the project owner brought back from a session with directie on **2026-08-19**: cut the explanatory prose out of the default view, make planning *inside* a themaperiode possible and legible down to the week and the day, let a teacher navigate weeks the way they navigate a calendar, show coverage progress while they are creating it, and stop offering a teacher goals that belong to another class.
**Covers FR:** FR-6.2, FR-6.3, FR-7.2 (E9-03/04/05), FR-3.2 (E9-07), FR-9.1 (E9-06), NFR-usability (E9-01/02).

V.1](../CONSTITUTION.md#article-v--coverage-must-be-provable-dekking) (dekking computed, never stored), [Art. XII](../CONSTITUTION.md) (colour + label).
**UX & a11y:** every story here is UI work, so every story here **starts with the `frontend-design` skill** and is looked at in a real browser at desktop *and* ~390px (the working agreement in [`CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md)). WCAG 2.2 AA; colour never alone.

## Why this epic exists, and what it is not

The build passed its own gates. Every screen in it was tested, audited and — since E0-10 — actually opened in a browser. **What none of those gates measure is whether a teacher wants to read the screen**, and the directie review says they do not: there is so much explanatory text that the data it explains is hard to find.

**This epic is not a redesign.** The themaoverzicht is explicitly fine and stays. The design system (ADR-0017), the token palette, the one-structural-hue rule and the Dutch copy catalogue all stand. What changes is **how much is on screen at once**, and **what a teacher can do once they are on it**.

> **A correction to a reading that would be easy to make here.** CR2 looks like new scope and is not. **FR-6.2 already reads *"De leerkracht kan thema's **en activiteiten** verslepen (drag-and-drop) tussen periodes"***, and **FR-6.3 leaves the view levels explicitly undecided** (*"exacte niveaus ter beslissing"*). Activiteiten have never been placeable in time in this build — E3 placed thema's and stopped. So E9-03/04 **close FR-6.2's second noun and answer FR-6.3**, rather than adding anything the functional analysis did not ask for. Whoever audits this should hold it to that standard and not to a lower one.

## The architectural decision that carries the epic — read before touching E9-03

**An activiteit is placed on a `DateOnly`, not on a planningsblok, and "week" is a rendering grouping of dates rather than a tier.**

The tempting design is a third `Planningsblokniveau` (`Week`, or `Dag`). It is wrong, on three independent grounds:

1. **Art. IX.3 and ADR-0013 fix the planning grid at the ratified two-tier themaperiode/subthemaperiode pair, configurable behind the E3-05 seam.** A week is a calendar unit, which is the exact assumption the domain enum is guarded against gaining — `Planningsblokniveau` has a test whose whole job is to fail when a calendar unit appears in it. Widening it to satisfy a UI request would compile in the assumption Art. XIV still leaves open.
2. **`Themaplaatsing.BlokStart` keys on a derived boundary that moves when the school edits its vakanties** (ADR-0020 §3), which is why `isVervallen` exists. A concrete teaching day does not move when a vakantie is edited — it either stays a school day or becomes a closure. Keying an activiteit on a block boundary would inherit a staleness problem it does not have.
3. **The two answer different questions.** A themaperiode answers *"in which stretch of the year does this thema live?"*; an activiteit on a Tuesday answers *"what am I doing on Tuesday?"*. Collapsing them into one ordinal space is the "two views disagree about the same period" defect the E3-02/E3-06 review had to repair twice.

So: a new aggregate member `Activiteitplaatsing { ActiviteitId, JaarplanId, Datum: DateOnly, Status, Volgorde }`, and the week view derives its columns from `Schooljaar` + `Schoolsluiting` the same way `Planningsblok` already derives `AantalOpenDagen`. **A subthema is not placed at all** — it inherits its span from the activiteiten placed under it, so there is no second thing to keep in step. Recorded as **[ADR-0023](../docs/adr/0023-activiteit-day-placement.md)** (accepted 2026-08-19); supersede rather than rewrite if it turns out wrong.

- [~] **E9-01 — One "Uitleg tonen" switch, default off** — *CR1. Owner ruling 2026-08-19. **Mechanism built 2026-08-19**, and it works. **But it removes ~4% of the prose, not most of it** — read the finding below before promising the directie anything.*

  *Done when:* a teacher who has never touched the switch sees no instructional prose on any screen; turning it on restores every sentence in place; the choice survives a reload; and the switch itself is reachable by keyboard and labelled.

A teacher must never lose the sentence that tells them what just went wrong, or that the dekkingscijfer is being withheld, because they turned off help they were reading as clutter.
  > **The measurement, so the story can be checked rather than argued about** (taken 2026-08-19): **113 keys of ≥110 chars, ~19 500 chars total** — 46 under `kalender`, 18 `parameters`, 15 `import`, 11 `dekking`, 7 `matching`, 7 `doelen`, 5 `themabeheer`, 2 `spine`, 1 each `binnenkort`/`ongekoppeld`. The other ~96% is **not** instructional prose.
