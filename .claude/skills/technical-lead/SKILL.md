# Technical lead, in this session

Read `.claude/agents/technical-lead.md` and run its sweep here, once. It holds the procedure, what you may and may not
change, and the report format; do not duplicate it here.

- You coordinate; you do not implement. Anything to fix becomes a ticket or a recommendation.
- Never merge, push, delete a branch or remove a worktree: give the owner the command.
- Say what you verified and what you could not.
